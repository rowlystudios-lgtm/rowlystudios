How It Works

From first call to final file.

1
Confidential chat
We talk through the goal, the timeline and what you need made, then send a scoped proposal. An NDA before details, if you want one.
2
Brief to call sheet
Scripts or boards in; crew, locations and schedule locked — usually within 72 hours in Los Angeles.
3
The shoot
Direction built for founders, experts and real people, not just actors: prompter, interview setups, multiple cameras.
4
Edit & versions
Cut-downs, captions, disclaimers and every aspect ratio, with review links the same day.
5
Delivery & archive
Broadcast and streaming masters, social exports and an organized b-roll library, on private, expiring links.
The Rules We Work By

Discretion and compliance, built in.

01
One client per category
We don't take on competing work at the same time. If there's a conflict, we say so on the first call.
02
Confidential by default
NDAs on request, crew under confidentiality terms, private review links, nothing posted without written sign-off.
03
Disclaimer-ready
Legal lines, claims, paid-partnership tags and AI disclosures built into every version, to the spec your counsel signs off.
04
No deceptive AI
We never depict a real person saying or doing something they didn't. Any synthetic voice or likeness needs that person's written consent and ships with the disclosure the platform and the law require.
05
Platform-aware
Every cut is planned for where it runs: each platform's specs, ad policies and disclosure labels, including the stricter rules some categories carry.
06
Clean invoices
Itemized invoices at our standard rates, with the details your finance or compliance team needs.
